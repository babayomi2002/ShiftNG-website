About ShiftNG

Accountability as a continuous national culture, not an occasional political event.

Our mission

ShiftNG exists to build an organised, informed, and engaged citizenry that drives accountability, transparency, and responsible governance across Nigeria. We empower citizens with credible data, technology, and collaborative civic tools to monitor public projects, track government performance, promote financial transparency, and strengthen democratic participation beyond election cycles.

As a strictly non-partisan, non-tribal, and non-religious civic platform, ShiftNG neither endorses nor fields political candidates. Instead, we strengthen democracy by making public information accessible, encouraging constructive citizen participation, and making accountability a continuous national culture rather than an occasional political event.

Our vision

To become Africa's leading citizen-powered civic intelligence platform, where every public project is visible, every public office is accountable, every election is transparently monitored, and every citizen has the tools to actively participate in shaping a more just, prosperous, and democratic society.

How it works

  • Anyone can read. You can browse offices, projects, news and courses without an account.
  • Sign in to take part. With a free account you can flag content that breaks the rules.
  • Learn, then report. After passing the required Academy courses you can post reports and photos on public projects, reply to others, and confirm what is still the case.
  • Moderated. Reports can be flagged by anyone signed in, and moderators remove what breaks the rules.

Who we are

ShiftNG is built in Nigeria for Nigerians. It is run by ShiftNG Analytics Limited (RC 10027494), an independent Nigerian company. ShiftNG is not a government app or website, and does not represent INEC, the National Assembly or any federal, state or local government. It is not affiliated with any political party.

Where the information comes from

  • Federal budget: the Budget Office of the Federation, budgetoffice.gov.ng.
  • State and local budgets: each state's approved budget documents, published on the state government's official website. Every project links to its document and page.
  • Elections and polling units: the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), inecnigeria.org, and its results portal (IReV), inecelectionresults.ng.
  • Elected officials: public records, including the National Assembly, nass.gov.ng, and state government and electoral commission websites.
