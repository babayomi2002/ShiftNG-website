Find your polling unit

Choose your state, local government and ward, or search by the polling unit's name or code. You will see the seats each unit votes for in 2027.

Where this comes from. Polling units are from INEC's published list, as held in ShiftNG on the last update. INEC can add, move or merge units before an election, so check with INEC before you go to vote.

Seats. We show a seat only when INEC's published lists confirm which constituency a ward belongs to. Where they do not yet, it says "Not confirmed yet" rather than a guess.
