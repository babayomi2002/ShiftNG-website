Know who serves you. Follow what is being built. Learn your rights.

ShiftNG is a civic app for Nigerians. It brings together the public offices that serve your ward, public projects in your area, trusted news and short courses on how government works, so accountability becomes an everyday habit, not just something for election season.

What is in the app

Choose your state, local government and ward, and ShiftNG shows what concerns you.

Civic

The offices that serve you

From the Presidency and the National Assembly to your state, your local government and your ward, with who holds each office where it is known.

Civic

Public projects near you

Follow roads, schools, clinics and other public projects. Add what you see on the ground with a photo, reply to other reports, or confirm that a problem is still the case. See the projects.

News

Trusted civic news

Accountability, politics, the economy and human rights, from established sources. Every story is checked by a person before it appears, and we mix sources on purpose so no single outlet or leaning dominates. Read the latest.

Academy

Short courses on your rights

Learn how government works and what you are entitled to. Passing the required courses lets you post reports, so every report has an informed person behind it.

Elections

Election information

What is being voted for in your area and when, with links to the electoral commission's own pages. See the 2027 elections or find your polling unit.

What we stand for

Non-partisan

ShiftNG is non-partisan, non-tribal and non-religious. We do not endorse or field candidates, and we take no side in any election.

Facts you can check

We point you to official sources and established outlets, and we say clearly when something comes from members of the public.

Accountable reporting

Reports come from signed-in people who have passed our courses. Anyone signed in can flag a report that breaks the rules, and moderators review it.

No advertising

The app is free and carries no advertising.
