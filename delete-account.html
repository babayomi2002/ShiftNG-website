Deleting your ShiftNG account

Last updated: 19 November 2026. This page explains how to delete your ShiftNG account and what happens to your information. It applies to the ShiftNG app on Android and iPhone.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open ShiftNG and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Your data.
  3. Tap Delete my account, choose what happens to your project reports, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. You can also tap Download my data first to keep a copy.

Without the app

Email privacy@shiftng.org from the email address you use for ShiftNG, with the subject "Delete my ShiftNG account". We check that the request comes from the account's address, delete the account, and confirm by email within one month (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

  • Your email address and sign-in.
  • Your profile: name and where you live.
  • Your Academy progress.
  • Posts you reported, and ward councillors you suggested.
  • The link between your app use and your account.
  • Your project reports, replies and photos, unless you choose to keep them as an anonymous public record that is no longer linked to you.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Election volunteers: your volunteer record, location stamps and the link between your reports and you are kept for 12 months after the election, the period in which election petitions and appeals are decided. Your phone number is deleted 30 days after the election, or at once if you withdraw. Checked result sheets stay as a public record of the election, without your name.
  • Audit record: a record of actions taken on your account is kept for 12 months, for security.
  • Backups: our encrypted backups are replaced within 35 days, after which no copy remains.
  • Complaints and legal claims: where content is the subject of one, a restricted copy may be kept until it is resolved.

See our Privacy and Cookie Policy for more, or write to privacy@shiftng.org. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
